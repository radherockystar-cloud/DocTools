Privacy Policy for DocTools

Last updated: October 2026

At DocTools (accessible from https://freedoctools.online), safeguarding the privacy of our visitors and users is one of our top priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by DocTools and how we utilize it to maintain a transparent, secure environment.

1. Zero Server-Side File Storage & Local Processing

The core architecture of DocTools is built entirely around client-side execution. When you upload, compress, resize, convert, or edit documents, images, signatures, and PDFs on our platform, your files never leave your personal device or terminal. All computations, image manipulation, background removal, and OCR scanning are performed locally inside your browser sandbox using modern Web APIs and JavaScript engines. Consequently, we do not store, copy, analyze, or archive your private files on any remote cloud server.

2. Log Files and Basic Traffic Analytics

Like standard web utility applications, DocTools follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertisements

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to freedoctools.online and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Our advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data. For easier access, we hyperlinked to their Privacy Policies below:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
