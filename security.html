Security Architecture

Last updated: October 2026

At DocTools, we treat security and data confidentiality as our highest engineering priority[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). When handling sensitive government exam documents, academic marksheets, personal photographs, and signature files, security cannot be an afterthought[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

1. Local In-Browser Processing (Client-Side Engine)

Traditional utility websites require you to upload your private files to a remote cloud server where they are processed, stored temporarily, and later deleted[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span). This creates major vulnerabilities for data leaks or unauthorized interception[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). DocTools completely eliminates this risk[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span). By leveraging advanced HTML5 Canvas APIs, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript execution, every compression, background removal, format conversion, and OCR extraction runs entirely inside your device's RAM[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).

2. Zero Cloud Uploads

Because your files never touch an external server or database, there is zero chance of network-based data theft or server logs retaining your sensitive paperwork[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Once you close or refresh your browser tab, all active working data is instantly cleared from your browser's temporary memory[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

3. Secure HTTPS Encryption

Our platform is served exclusively over secure HTTPS connections (TLS/SSL encryption), ensuring that all static assets and scripts loaded onto your terminal are fully authenticated and protected against man-in-the-middle tampering[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).

4. Reporting Security Concerns

If you ever discover a vulnerability or have technical inquiries regarding our security protocols, please feel free to report it directly to our development team at radherockystar@gmail.com[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
