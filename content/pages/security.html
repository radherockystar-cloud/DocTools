People use FreeDocTools with photos, signatures, marksheets and ID documents, so we want to be clear about what happens to them. This page describes how the site is designed, and also what it cannot protect against.

Files stay on your device

The tools open and change your files inside your browser. They are not sent to a FreeDocTools server, and there is no upload step. When you close or refresh the page, the working data is released from the browser's memory. Files you download are saved by your browser to your device like any other download.

What is downloaded when you use a tool

  • The page, its scripts and styles are loaded from our hosting provider.
  • The background remover downloads an AI model the first time you use it.
  • The text extractor downloads language data for the language you select.

These downloads bring data to your device. Your files do not travel in the other direction.

Encrypted connection

The site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts the connection between your browser and the site and helps prevent the pages and scripts from being changed on the way to you.

What this does not protect against

Running in your browser removes the risk of a server storing your file, but your device and browser still matter:

  • Browser extensions can read pages you visit. Use a private window with extensions disabled for sensitive documents.
  • On a shared or public computer, delete downloaded files and empty the recycle bin or downloads folder when you finish.
  • Files you download or send onward are outside our control.
  • Keep your browser and operating system updated.

Tips for sensitive documents

  • Process ID documents on your own phone or computer, not on a public one.
  • Do not share compressed or converted copies of your ID in chat apps unless you trust the person receiving them.
  • If a portal allows it, remove details you do not need to share before uploading.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem, please email radherockystar@gmail.com with the subject "Security". Describe the page, the steps to reproduce it and the effect you saw. Please do not publish details until we have had a reasonable chance to fix it.
