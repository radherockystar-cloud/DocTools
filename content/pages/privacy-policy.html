This policy explains what information FreeDocTools (https://freedoctools.online) handles when you use the site, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and specific.

1. Your files

The tools on FreeDocTools process images and documents inside your browser. When you pick a file, it is read and changed on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and we cannot see, store or share it. Closing or refreshing the page clears the working data from your browser memory.

Two tools download extra files from public content delivery networks (CDNs) the first time you use them: the background remover downloads an AI model, and the text extractor downloads language data. These downloads do not include your photo, but the CDN can see ordinary request details such as your IP address.

2. Information we and our providers collect

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider, Vercel, handles requests to the site and may log technical details such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time.
  • Site analytics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see general information such as the page visited, referring site, country and device or browser type. According to Vercel, this service does not use cookies and does not track you across websites.
  • Messages you send us. If you email or message us, we receive your contact details and what you write, and we use them only to reply and improve the site.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, phone number or address through the tools.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site stores one preference in your browser's local storage: whether you chose light or dark mode. It stays on your device and is not sent to us. Google AdSense may set its own cookies, described below.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform. This advertising is separate from the files you process, which Google never receives from us.

You can control personalised advertising:

  • Manage or turn off personalised ads in Google Ads Settings.
  • Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info/choices.

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. Other third-party resources

To display pages, your browser loads some resources from other companies, such as fonts from Google Fonts and a styling script from the Tailwind CSS CDN. Loading them reveals your IP address and browser details to those companies, as with any website. Each has its own privacy policy.

6. Payments

FreeDocTools does not currently charge for its tools and does not collect or process payments or card and bank details.

7. Children

The site is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
  • Use a private window if you are on a shared device.
  • Manage ad personalisation with the links in section 4.
  • Ask us a question or request about your information by email.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, for example by adding a new tool that needs a server, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

10. Contact
