FreeDocTools is a set of free image and PDF tools that run in your web browser. You can compress a photo to an exact size, remove a background, merge PDFs, extract text from a scan and more, without creating an account and without installing anything.

Why this site exists

Online applications for exams, jobs, banks and colleges often ask for a photo under a strict file size, a signature in a specific format, or documents merged into one PDF. Doing this usually means installing software or uploading personal documents to a website you know nothing about. FreeDocTools was built to make these everyday jobs quick, and to do them on your own device.

Who builds it

FreeDocTools is built and maintained by Rocky Star 474, an independent developer. It is not affiliated with any government body, exam authority or bank. You can reach the developer through the contact page.

How the tools work

The tools use features built into your browser, such as the canvas for images and JavaScript libraries for PDFs and text recognition. When you choose a file, it is opened and changed on your own device. We do not receive a copy of it.

Two tools need to download extra data the first time you use them: the background remover downloads an AI model, and the text extractor downloads language data for the language you select. These downloads are public files. Your photo is not part of those requests. The privacy policy and the security page explain this in more detail.

What we promise, and what we do not

  • The tools are free to use and do not need registration.
  • We do not sell your files, because we never receive them.
  • We explain limits honestly on each tool page. For example, the image compressor always saves JPG, and OCR works best on printed text rather than handwriting.
  • We cannot guarantee that a particular exam or government portal will accept a file. Rules change, so always check the official notification.

How the site is paid for

Hosting and development take time and money. To cover this, the site may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Ads are set up by Google and are not connected to the files you process. See the privacy policy for how advertising cookies work and how to control them.

Feedback

If a tool fails, gives a poor result or is missing a feature you need, please tell us on the contact page. Tell us what you did, which browser and phone or computer you used, and what went wrong. Real problems reported by users are the main way the tools get better.
