What to include when something does not work

  1. The name of the tool and the page address you were on.
  2. What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  3. Your device and browser, for example "Android phone, Chrome".
  4. The size and type of the file (JPG, PNG, PDF) and any error message you saw.

Please do not send your personal documents, ID cards or photos by email unless we ask for a specific sample. A screenshot of the error is usually enough.

Quick answers

Is my file uploaded to your server?

No. Files are processed in your browser. Read the privacy policy and the security page for details.

Do the tools cost money or need an account?

No. All tools are free and work without registration.

The result was rejected by an exam or government portal. Why?

Each portal sets its own rules for file size, dimensions and format, and these rules change. Check the latest official notification and set the tool to match it. If you still cannot meet a rule, send us the exact requirement and we will look at it.

Can I request a new tool?

Yes. Send us a short description of what you need and the situation where you need it.

How do I report a privacy or security concern?

Email radherockystar@gmail.com with the subject "Security" or "Privacy" and describe the issue.
